Unit 1
Let's talk Part A
Li Shan: Hi, Su Nan! We're back to school again.
Su Nan: It's so great to see you! What lessons will we have this term?
Li Shan: We'll have Chinese, math, English ... and labor education.
Su Nan: Labor education! That's great!
Su Nan: Hi, Miss Li! What will we learn in labor education class?
Miss Li: The Mid-Autumn Festival is coming. You'll learn to make dumplings, milk tea and mooncakes.
Su Nan: Sounds good! I like mooncakes.
Miss Li: Me too.
Let's read
There are four seasons in a year. Each season has three months. September, October and November make autumn. In the middle of autumn, the Chinese people have an important festival — the Mid-Autumn Festival.
On that day, many families will get together and have a big dinner. In my family, Mom will make mooncakes for us. They are the important food for the festival. My grandma will tell us a story about "Chang'e and her rabbit". In the evening, we will eat mooncakes and enjoy the bright full moon together.

Colin: That's really interesting!
