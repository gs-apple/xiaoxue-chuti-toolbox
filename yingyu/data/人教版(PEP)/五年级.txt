Unit 2
Let's talk PartA
John is talking with his mum. Listen and write T (True) or F (False).
1. They are talking about John's school project. (    )
2. John wants to go to the old people's home. (    )
Chen Jie: Hi, Mike. You look sad. What's the matter?
Mike: It's about our school project. John wants to go to the children's centre, but I want to clean up the park.
Chen Jie: I see. So you don't agree with each other?
Mike: That's right. And we have to finish our project by Monday. I'm so worried!
Chen Jie: Why not do both? I can help. We can work together.
Mike: That's a good idea!
Let's talk PartB
Miss White: What's the matter, John? You look worried.
John: It's about our project. Mike and I want to do different things.
Miss White: I see. It's important to listen to each other.
John: Yes, Miss White. I need to talk to Mike. But I'm afraid he's angry with me.
Miss White: Look, here he comes. You can talk to him now.
Mike: John, I'm sorry. Let's do both projects! Chen Jie can work with us.
John: That's great!
Binbin sees Mike. Listen and write T (True) or F (False).
1. Binbin wants to clean up the park. (    )
2. Mike and Binbin have different ideas. (    )
Read and write
Dear Mike,
I am happy at school. I have many friends. I play with them in the playground, and we share ideas in class. We laugh and study together all day. I enjoy school.
But my family just moved to a new home. I have no friends there. I feel sad. I don't want to tell my parents. I don't want them to feel bad.
What can I do? Can you help me?
Yours, Jim

.表达句
Hi, Mike. You look sad. What's the matter? || 嗨，迈克。你看起来不开心。怎么回事？
It's about our school project. || 是关于我们学校的项目。
And we have to finish our project by Monday. || 并且我们得在星期一之前完成项目。

Unit 3
Let's talk PartA
It is Monday night. John is talking with his grandpa. Listen and tick the classes you hear.
Grandpa: What's your favourite day at school, John?
John: Wednesday.
Grandpa: What do you have on Wednesdays?
John: I have Chinese, maths, music and PE.
Grandpa: Maths is not easy.
John: No, but I really like my maths teacher!
Grandpa: That's good.
Grandpa: Is music your favourite subject?
John: Yes. I really like singing. And I like PE too.
Grandpa: Great! Wednesday is your favourite day.
Grandpa: But I like Tuesdays and Fridays.
John: Why, Grandpa?
Grandpa: Because I have my taijiquan class!
Let's talk PartB
Miss White: What do you usually do at the weekend, children?
Zhang Peng: I often play football. Sometimes I play Chinese chess with my father.
Miss White: That's nice.
Sarah: My mother works at the weekend, so I often cook lunch for my family.
Miss White: Good girl! Does your mother work on both Saturdays and Sundays?
Sarah: Yes, she does.
Chen Jie: I do my homework on Saturdays. And I usually have pipa lessons on Sundays.
Miss White: Great! Have a nice weekend, everyone!
Miss White and Mr Li are talking. Listen and fill in the blanks.
1. They are talking about ________ activities.
2. ________ often cooks. ________ usually goes to the park with his dog.
Read and write
Miss White asks the students what they do after school. Here are the answers from Li Xia and Eric.
Dear Miss White,
I play ping-pong with Binbin on Mondays and Thursdays. On Tuesdays, I cook dinner because my mother works on Tuesday nights. On Wednesdays and Fridays, I usually read books. At the weekend, I often play with my friends in the park or visit my grandparents. I am busy, but I always do my homework on time!
Li Xia
Dear Miss White,
On Monday, Tuesday and Friday evenings, I play football. I often watch TV on Wednesdays and Thursdays. On Saturdays and Sundays, I play basketball and go running. I am so busy. I don't have much time to study.
Eric

Lotus flowers are very beautiful. They have many colours. Lotus plants grow in lakes around the world. It is also a great plant to eat.

.课文
Unit 6
Let's talk PartA
Mike's family are in a nature park. They are talking with Mr Liu, the tour guide. Listen and write T (True) or F (False).
1. It is a cloudy day. (    )
2. There are many animals in the nature park. (    )
Mike's dad: Wow! That lake is really lovely!
Mr Liu: Yes. There are also many rivers in the nature park.
Mike's dad: Is there a waterfall in the forest?
Mr Liu: Yes, there is. You'll see a beautiful one today!
Mike: Great! Are there any famous mountains in the nature park?
Mr Liu: Yes, there are. We'll visit one tomorrow. There are many flowers too. And there are rabbits!
Let's talk PartB
Mike and his dad are talking about the weather. Listen and circle.
1. Today's weather is good/bad.
2. There will be rain/snow this afternoon.
Mr Liu: I'm sorry. The wind is very strong outside. We can't go on our trip this morning.
Mike's mum: I agree. Strong winds are often dangerous.
Mike's dad: There will be heavy rain in the afternoon too. Mike, stay in the hotel with Mum and close the windows. I'll go to buy some umbrellas and raincoats.
Mike's mum: There is a small shop in the hotel. You can buy them there.
Mike's dad: OK.
Mr Liu: Please don't worry! It'll be a fine day tomorrow.
Read and write
Things you should know before mountain hiking
Hiking in the mountains can be a lot of fun, but there are some things you should know:
Be careful of bad weather. Don't go hiking in bad weather! In summer there is heavy rain and in winter there is heavy snow.
Bring enough water. In the mountains there is often little clean water, so bring enough water with you.
Wear good hiking shoes and the right clothes. It is very important to wear the right shoes. It is often windy in the mountains too, so bring a jacket with you.
Tell people about your trip. Tell your parents and friends before you go. They need to know where you are.
