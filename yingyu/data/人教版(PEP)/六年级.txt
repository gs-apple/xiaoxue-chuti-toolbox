Unit 1
Let's talk PartA
Zhang Peng: Hi, Leo! How was your weekend?
Leo: Not too bad, thanks. And you?
Zhang Peng: It was great! I climbed the Great Wall.
Leo: Wow! The Great Wall is amazing.
Zhang Peng: Yes, it is! It's about 21,000 kilometres long. What did you do, Leo?
Leo: I visited the "Gingerbread House" last Saturday.
Zhang Peng: What did you do there? Eat gingerbread?
Leo: Ha! Ha! No! It's a beautiful train station in New Zealand.
Zhang Peng: That sounds interesting. Please send me some pictures.
Let's talk PartB
Amy: Where did you go over the summer holidays, Binbin?
Binbin: I went to Xi'an with my family.
Sarah: I like Xi'an. What did you do there?
Binbin: We ate some famous Xi'an noodles. Then we went to see the Terracotta Warriors.
Sarah: Wow! I really want to go there! How was it?
Binbin: Amazing! There are over seven thousand clay warriors and horses.
Amy: I saw pictures on the internet. What else did you do?
Binbin: Well, we walked around the old city and rode bikes on the old city wall.
Amy: That sounds great fun!
Read and write
My trip to the Jinggangshan Revolution Museum was amazing! I walked around the museum for hours and saw many old books and photos. The volunteers told great stories about the Red Army. The stories were really inspiring. I learnt a lot. Do you want to learn more about the Red Army? You should go there!
This was a great place for a summer trip! We climbed the Jinggang Mountains and took many photos of the bamboo forest. We tried delicious pumpkin soup and red rice in a nice restaurant too. The Jinggang Mountains are only about 1.5 hours from the airport by bus. I want to go there again!

Unit 2
Let's talk PartA
Mike: How was your Mid-Autumn Festival holiday, Binbin?
Binbin: It was great! My family and I had a really nice day.
Mike: What did you do? Did you take a trip?
Binbin: No, we didn't. My aunt's family visited us.
Mike: Did you eat mooncakes?
Binbin: Yes, we did. We enjoyed some mooncakes and fruit. Grandpa told us some stories about the festival.
Mike: Wow! Can I join you next year?
Binbin: Sure. You can also join us for the Spring Festival. We can paste fu and eat jiaozi together.
Mike: Great! I can't wait!
Let's talk PartB
Chen Jie: How was your weekend, Amy?
Amy: It was great. My mother and I went to a marathon on Sunday. She worked as a volunteer. I watched the race.
Chen Jie: What did you like about it?
Amy: I liked watching the runners. They ran very fast. I cheered for them.
Binbin: Nice! And you, Chen Jie? What did you do last weekend?
Chen Jie: I joined an online book fair. I liked it very much.
Binbin: What did you like about it?
Chen Jie: We shared ideas and talked about different books.
Binbin: That sounds fun!
Read and write
Love boats? Like exciting races? The Dragon Boat Festival is coming. Come and join us!
The notice about the dragon boat race finally came out last Friday. I was excited because my father was on a race team!
In the morning, my dad woke up early and went to the river. My mum and I went later and waited for the race. Then the judge counted down. The race began! I ran and cheered for my dad. His boat was very fast and his team won the race! They all sang their team song together. They were so happy!
In the evening, our family made zongzi and had a big dinner for the festival, and for the winner, my dad! It was the best Dragon Boat Festival ever!

Unit 3
Let's talk PartA
Mike: Hello?
Binbin: Hi, Mike. It's Binbin. I'm going to the science museum tomorrow. Are you going?

Binbin: Oh dear! How do you feel?
Mike: I feel ill. My head hurts and I have a runny nose.
Binbin: I'm sorry to hear that. Maybe you should see a doctor.
Mike: I know. My mum is going to take me to hospital this afternoon.
Binbin: That's good. Take care and get well soon!
Mike: Thanks.
Let's talk PartB
Miss White: Class, now we're going to discuss health. First, what is health?
Chen Jie: Health is having a good body and feeling happy.
Miss White: Thank you, Chen Jie. So, how can we live a healthy life?
Sarah: We need to eat healthy food. We should exercise too.
Miss White: That's right. What do you think, Binbin?
Binbin: I think our family and friends are also very important.
Chen Jie: Yes, it's always good to talk to our family and friends.
Miss White: Good job, everyone!
Read and write
Liu Jia was crying. So Miss White asked her, "What's the matter, Liu Jia? You look sad." Liu Jia answered, "I'm feeling down. My good friend Lucy moved to another city."
Miss White listened carefully and told Liu Jia, "Think about happy things. Call your friend or send her some emails."
"I tried that, but I still feel unhappy," said Liu Jia.
Miss White gave her a glass of water. "Maybe you can do some exercise. Exercise may cheer you up!"
"Join some after-school clubs and make new friends! You can talk and have fun with them," added Miss White.
"I will try. Thank you, Miss White," said Liu Jia.
Dear Miss White,
I often make video calls with my friend now. I also joined a ping-pong club and made some new friends. I feel much better. Thank you so much!
Liu Jia

Unit 4

Let's talk PartB
Mum: How much pocket money do you have?
Sarah: I have one hundred and forty-six yuan.
Mum: What else are you going to buy?
Sarah: A lot. But first I'm going to buy a pair of sunglasses for Grandpa.
Mum: That's so nice of you!
Sarah: I also want to buy a microscope.
Mum: Good idea! But microscopes are expensive.
Sarah: I have some lucky money from the Spring Festival, but I still need to save more.
Mum: Maybe you can wait for a sale!
Read and write
Manage money well
Chen Jie has her special way to manage money. She writes a spending plan every Sunday. Every week, she gets her pocket money. She also gets some money from selling old things. She keeps her money in three money jars. Jar 1 is money to spend. She uses this money to buy goods, like books. She also uses it to pay for services, like haircuts. Jar 2 is money to share. She uses this money to buy gifts, for example, birthday gifts for her family. Jar 3 is money to save. Chen Jie puts money there to save up for big things, like a new bike. She really manages her money well!

Unit 5
Let's talk PartA
Mr Liu: Hi, children. Let's talk about our planet Earth. What can you see in the sky?
Mike: The sky is often blue in the daytime. We can see the Sun and clouds. We can also see birds and planes.
Amy: At night, we can see the Moon and the stars.
Mr Liu: Very good. But what can we see from outer space? Let's look at some photos from a space station.
Binbin: Wow! We can see mountains, oceans and clouds.
Chen Jie: There are large oceans on Earth. It looks like a big blue marble.
Mike: It's so beautiful. I want to know more about our Mother Earth!
Let's talk PartB
Mike: Hey, Chen Jie. What are you reading about?
Chen Jie: Mars.
Oliver: Mars? What does it say?
Chen Jie: It talks about things on Mars.
Oliver: Wow! Is there any water on Mars?
Chen Jie: Yes, there is. There is soil too.
Oliver: Oh! Are there any aliens?
Chen Jie: I don't think so. They say there aren't any living things.
Oliver: I see.

Read and write
Chinese astronauts are called taikonauts. Their life in space is special.
The taikonauts get up early in the morning. But getting ready in space is different. They can't use water to wash their faces. So taikonauts have to use a wet cloth. They brush their teeth with a special toothpaste and have to "eat" it!
Each day the taikonauts are very busy. They do a lot of work. For example, they grow vegetables and rice. Sometimes, they also give classes to children and answer questions about space.
It is hard work. The taikonauts must stay healthy. Every afternoon they do exercise to keep healthy and strong. But their job is also very interesting. They can see the sunrise and sunset 16 times a day. The view is amazing! There is no other job like this one.

Unit 6
Let's talk PartA
Binbin: Hi, Mike. Any ideas about our project?
Mike: Maybe we can talk about water power.
Binbin: OK. We use water every day. Let's find out more about water power.
Mike: What do you know about it?
Binbin: Running water can make electricity. We can get power from the sea too.
Mike: What else do you want to know about water?
Binbin: Well, where does water come from? How do we make power from water?
Mike: Good questions. Let's go to the library. We can work on it together.
Binbin: Good idea!
Let's talk PartB
Mrs Green: We use energy every day. But many resources are running out. What should we do?
Mike: We should save energy.
Mrs Green: Yes, but how can we save energy?
Binbin: We can change our energy use.
Mrs Green: Can you give us an example, Binbin?
Binbin: Sure. We can take quick showers. That will save energy and water.
Mike: I agree. We can also use LED lighting.
Binbin: And we should always unplug our computers and TVs.
Mrs Green: Great! Small changes can make a big difference.
Read and write
Mother Earth is running out of resources! We should all try to save energy and save our Earth. What can we do? Here are our top three ideas.
Take a quick shower.
Wash all your dirty clothes together, not one at a time.
Wash fruit and vegetables, then use the water again.
Use LED lighting.
Turn off your TV or computer and unplug it.
Use both sides of paper.
Reuse old bottles. You can put pencils in them.
